Mamichess Puzzle Prime
The goal for the Queen (♕) is to checkmate the opposing King (♚) by forcing it onto the "grave" square (💀, d3: the top-right square). Queen moves first. The King will try its best to avoid the grave or capture the Queen. Queen has a forced win.
