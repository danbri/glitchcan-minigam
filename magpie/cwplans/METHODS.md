### Crawl and store-finder method (all crawlers)

- Identify: User-Agent `glitchcan-cwplans/0.1 (https://github.com/danbri/glitchcan-minigam)`; the headless render adds
  that token to a desktop Chrome string.
- robots.txt per RFC 9309: the group for `glitchcan-cwplans`, else `*`; longest match wins, Allow wins a tie; a 4xx
  robots.txt allows all; a 5xx or unreachable robots.txt disallows all; checked again on every redirect to a new host.
- One request at a time per host with a gap (1.5 s for the plain crawl, 2 s for the headless render, about 1 s for the
  store-page check), a few hosts at once, a timeout, and backoff on 429, 5xx, timeouts and resets (Retry-After first).
- Skipped by rule: FSA ratings pages, OSM, Wikidata, Companies House and Land Registry pages (their data comes from
  APIs and bulk files), Wikipedia (CC BY-SA, share-alike), social networks, and the live canarywharf.com (it answers
  scripts with an Imperva challenge; its pages are read as Internet Archive `id_` copies).
- Kept: structured data (schema.org JSON-LD, microdata, RDFa), titles, OpenGraph fields cut to 200 characters,
  links, feeds. No other page text. Rendered pages stay local (gitignored).
- A page counts for a branch only when it names the place (postcode, street, mall); a result that does not is reported
  as such, not dropped silently.

### Hand curation (general rules)

- A hand decision is written to a file the tools read (`fsa-decisions.json`, `cwg-decisions.json`,
  `storelocator-manual.json`, `data/sourced-levels.json`, `registry/sources/facades/work/rois.json`, the `JUDGEMENT`
  table in `tools/build-towers.py`), with its reason. The tools apply it on every run; nobody edits an output by hand.
- Research catalogues (`feeds/`, `feeds/underground/`, `docklands/facts.json`) keep the URL, the fetch date, the
  licence as read, and a short quote where a value is taken. Copyright plans are looked at, not copied.
- Where a method was run as a one-off script that is not committed, its hand step in `pipeline.json` says so
  (`gap`). Commit the script next time the step is run.

### Licence decisions

The licence of each source is in `data-register.json` `sources` (readable in `DATA-REGISTER.md`), with the reason
for each decision in the source's README. The open decisions and refusals are in the hand step `licence-decisions`
below (area "Provenance export").

#### 22. `build-data` (tools/build-data.mjs)

- Command: `node magpie/cwplans/tools/build-data.mjs`
- Method: Builds the corridor model (Canada Water): a 10 m terrain grid, building footprints with heights, water levels, railways with tunnel chains and open cuts, roads, and stations and Wikidata places, in local metres around E0 535400, N0 179400.
- Rules:
  - Terrain: median of the DTM in a 7 x 7 m window every 10 m.

#### 23. `build-docklands` (tools/build-docklands.mjs)

- Command: `node --max-old-space-size=8000 magpie/cwplans/tools/build-docklands.mjs`
- Method: Builds the Docklands 3D model (terrain, water, greens, buildings and parts, roads, railways, tunnels, places, flood defences, riverbed) into area.js, and the Canary Wharf underground detail (basements, indoor ways, level-tagged points, published structures) into under.js, in local metres around E0 537550, N0 180300.
- Rules:
  - Terrain: median of the DTM in a 7 x 7 m window every 20 m.
  - Building height: OSM height; else LiDAR 90th percentile of DSM - DTM (at least 6 cells, at least 2 m) unless under half of building:levels x 3 m; else Wikidata P2048 (outlines only); else building:levels x 3 m + 1 m ('newer' when LiDAR exists); else LiDAR; else 6 m (0 for construction, roof, ruins).
  - An outline that contains building parts is drawn by its parts; a part's base is its min_height, else building:min_level x 3 m.
  - Outside the Canary Wharf focus box, outlines under 12 m2 are dropped and rings are simplified at 0.6 m (0.2 m inside).
  - Basements: OSM building:levels:underground, else Wikidata P1139.
  - Floors (focus box only): OSM building:levels (minus building:min_level on a part), else Wikidata P1101.
  - Water level: 25th percentile of the DTM inside the area; areas under 40 m2 (water) or 300 m2 (greens) are dropped.
  - Bridge points more than 20 m above the DTM are taken as covered by a roof and interpolated from the nearest uncovered points on the same way.
  - Tunnel chains for rail, road and foot tunnels; portals at the open level; open cut where the DTM is more than 6 m below the ground 25-35 m around.
  - Riverbed: each UKHO sounding in the box as z_cd minus the chart datum offset interpolated by longitude between 3.20 m and 3.35 m.
  - Flood defence crest: actual_ucl, else actual_dcl, else design_ucl; lines without a crest are dropped.
  - Published levels from data/sourced-levels.json are tunnel controls (nearest point within 150 m); structures with footprint_osm or water_name are drawn from their OSM way or water area.
  - Wikidata places: classes matching the KEEP pattern; classes matching memorial, war memorial, grave or cemetery are left out.
  - Level tags are read by tools/osm-values.mjs osmLevels.

### 3. Towers

#### 74. `build-towers` (tools/build-towers.py)

- Command: `/opt/towers-venv/bin/python magpie/cwplans/tools/build-towers.py`
- Method: Fits tiered massing (stacked flat tiers, or a pyramid, cone or sloped top where that fits clearly better) to the towers of 100 m or more, from the 1 m EA LiDAR surface model, for the 3D page.
- Rules:
  - Primary survey: the 2022 composite (flights of 2017-12 to 2018-01 and 2020-12-12); the 2018 flight where judgement says so (Novotel, Dollar Bay: under half their roof cells returned in 2020).
  - Dropouts (8 m under the 75th percentile of their 5 x 5 m) filled by a 3 x 3 m grey closing.
  - OSM outline shifted by whole metres (at most 4) onto the LiDAR building.
  - Roof levels: 1-D Gaussian mixture, k by BIC + 2 k ln n; levels closer than 3 m merge.
  - Outlines: open and close, holes under 60 m2 filled, pieces under 16 m2 removed, marching squares, Douglas-Peucker 0.8 m, edges snapped to the dominant direction within 8 degrees.
  - Towers not standing at the 2020 flight keep their OSM outline (status not_in_survey).
  - Hand corrections are listed in the tool (JUDGEMENT) and in each record with its reason.
  - The 2022 composite merges only the 2017-12/2018-01 and 2020-12-12 flights: it is not an independent check of 2020; the independent check is the 2018 flight, for towers finished by January 2018.
  - Composite cells with no return (a gap filled from near the ground) are no data.
  - Roof RMSE is reported on roof cells only.
- Inputs:
  - `data/raw/dsm/<product>-<year>-<res>-<tile>.zip` (local, not committed)
  - [docklands/data/area.js](docklands/data/area.js)
  - [atlas/data/atlas.json](atlas/data/atlas.json)
  - [registry/buildings.json](registry/buildings.json)
- Outputs:
  - [docklands/data/towers.json](docklands/data/towers.json)
- Network: no; deterministic: yes; kind: derive; after: `build-docklands`, `build-atlas`, `fetch-dsm`
- Hand judgement: JUDGEMENT in tools/build-towers.py: the primary survey per tower (2018 for Novotel cwb-0701 and Dollar Bay cwb-0737), levels added by hand (Pan Peninsula cwb-0801: 6.5 and 21.5 m read off the height histogram), a ground level for the height check (8 Canada Square cwb-0417: 9.7 m OD); SKIP lists outlines that are not towers (cwb-0418, cwb-0523). Each reason is in the tool and in the tower record.
- Note: Python with numpy, scipy, scikit-learn, scikit-image, rasterio, shapely, Pillow in /opt/towers-venv. Writes contact sheets to data/raw/towers/ (not committed).

#### 66. `build-registry` (tools/build-registry.mjs)

- Command: `node magpie/cwplans/tools/build-registry.mjs`
- Method: Builds the Canary Wharf building registry: one record per OSM building outline with a stable cwb- id, its parts, occupants (OSM, FSA, Wikidata, chain-store branches, the Canary Wharf Group directory, the regulatory and public registers), owners, postcodes, Wikidata facts, and company and property joins.

  - OSM occupants: named features with a shop, food and drink, office, amenity, leisure, tourism, healthcare, craft or transport role, placed in the outline that contains them or the nearest outline within 12 m; artworks, information boards, viewpoints and attractions are features, not occupants.   - Wikidata: an OSM wikidata tag wins; else the one building-like item whose coordinate is in the outline (within 5 m) and no other building-like item is inside it; an item already named by an OSM tag is not attached to a second outline; other items inside are occupants 'located here'.

#### 57. `build-trees` (tools/build-trees.mjs)

- Command: `node --max-old-space-size=6000 magpie/cwplans/tools/build-trees.mjs`
- Method: Joins the tree inventories, OSM trees and the Trees Outside Woodland lone-tree crowns into one list of trees in model metres, with OS Open Greenspace sites, OSM green areas and TOW canopy groups as green rings.
- Rules:
  - Box: BNG E 532400-539900, N 176700-182300 (fetch-docklands.mjs BOX_BNG); WGS84 to BNG through OSTN15; x = E - 537550, z = -(N - 180300); every value rounded to 0.5 m.
  - Ethics (CLAUDE.md, Data ethics): a tree keeps only position, taxon (scientific and common name), height, crown spread, source and record id. Notes, sponsorship, memorial, dedication, inscription, address, owner and maintainer fields are never read into the payload; each source lists its dropped fields in sources[].dropped.
  - Tree precedence: GLA public realm inventory first; then TPO points (planning.data), OSM natural=tree nodes and TOW Lone Tree crowns, each added only if no tree already kept lies within 3 m (TOW: within the crown radius, at least 3 m).
  - GLA class values ("05 to 10 m") become the middle of the class; "Not recorded" is null. Species "Unknown Tree Species" and common name "Plant" give no species.
  - TPO: records with an end date are left out; the taxon is read only from Tower Hamlets names of the form "Common (Scientific)"; Southwark names (site descriptions, URLs) are dropped.
  - OSM: species, else taxon, else genus (with the :en names); height under 60 m and diameter_crown under 40 m kept.
  - TOW Lone Tree: centroid of the polygon, height = maxht, crown = diameter of a circle with the polygon area (tow_area_m). Group of Trees, Small Woodland and NFI OHC polygons become greens with meanht.
  - Greens are clipped to the box (Sutherland-Hodgman) and simplified (Douglas-Peucker 0.5 m; TOW 1.5 m, its outlines follow the 1 m LiDAR grid); OSM multipolygon inner rings and shapefile holes are kept as holes.
  - Network for the source files: see fetch-trees.
- Inputs:
  - `data/raw/trees/gla-public-realm-trees-2025Nov.csv` (local, not committed)
  - `data/raw/trees/opgrsp_essh_tq.zip` (local, not committed)
  - `data/raw/trees/planning-data-tree.json` (local, not committed)
  - `data/raw/trees/tow-london-box.json.gz` (local, not committed)

#### 53. `fetch-sky` (tools/fetch-sky.mjs)

- Method: Fetches the star catalogue, the constellation lines, satellite orbital elements, hourly weather and tide-gauge readings for the Sky panel of the 3D page (docklands/sky.js) and writes small JSON snapshots; the weather and tide files cover the London date given and the next day (the owner's photo evening, 3 October 2026).
- Rules:
  - Stars: Bright Star Catalogue rows with a J2000 position and V <= 5.5 (2,887); columns from the CDS ReadMe: HR 1-4, RA 76-83, Dec 84-90, Vmag 103-107, B-V 110-114; RA and Dec to 0.001 degree, V and B-V to 0.01; a missing B-V becomes 0.6; sorted by V.

  - Satellites: the three requests merged, one record per NORAD catalogue number; OMM fields kept as published. CelesTrak keeps no history, so a snapshot holds the elements current at fetch time (run within a day or two of the date). One request per group per run (CelesTrak: at most one download per 2 hours).
  - Weather: the forecast API's past hours (best_match model, UK Met Office UKV first for London; it has visibility, which the ERA5 archive lacks); values as published, hourly, London time.

The page (sky.js cleanGauges) leaves out a reading whose difference from the nearest other gauge departs more than 0.45 m from the median of that difference over 2 h either side (fault F21), then interpolates linearly by chainage along data/river.json between the gauges. The EA has no tidal gauge between Tower Pier and Charlton (station search of 2026-10-04, 15 km round the estate).
  - Star names: rows of IAU-CSN whose designation is an HR number present in stars.json (324 of 451 names); columns found by the positions of the header titles (Name/Diacritics, Designation, Con), because names can hold spaces; the diacritic form is kept.
  - Clouds: the EUMETSAT cloud mask PNG kept as sent. The page (sky.js) reads white pixels (R, G, B > 200) as cloud, takes the mean over 60 km round the box centre, and raises each layer's Open-Meteo cover by 0.9 x (local mask - mean), clamped to 0..1, so the mask places the cloud and Open-Meteo keeps the amount; the mask moves with 2.2 x the 10 m wind for up to 90 minutes from its slot. - Method: Every new or changed tool updates its pipeline.json activity in the same commit (used, generated, method, rules, network, deterministic, after, area, faults, judgement); every new data file gets a data-register.json entry in the same commit; then check-data-register.mjs --write regenerates DATA-REGISTER.md, METHODS.md and pipeline.jsonld.
- Rules:
  - The check fails on an unlisted tool, an empty method or rules, an unknown area or fault id, an unregistered data file, and a page that shows OSM data without the attribution link.
  - Source identifiers are kept as they come (OSM type/id, QID, UPRN, TOID, FHRS id, company number, cwb- id): they become IRIs.
- Inputs: none
- Outputs:
  - [pipeline.json](pipeline.json)
- Decisions recorded in: pipeline.json; skills/docklands-data-curation/ACTIVITY-LOG.md

## Libraries and tests

- [tools/lib.mjs](tools/lib.mjs): HTTP get with the project User-Agent; QLever (paced, one call at a time, retried on 429 and 5xx) and query.wikidata.org SPARQL helpers; EA LiDAR WCS URLs; WGS84 to BNG through the OSTN15 grid (reads data/raw/uk_os_OSTN15_NTv2_OSGBtoETRS.tif); LiDAR tile mosaics; polygon helpers (area, point in polygon, box clip, cells, earcut mesh, ring joining, Douglas-Peucker, delta encoding); applyControls (published levels from data/sourced-levels.json as tunnel controls).
- [tools/registry-lib.mjs](tools/registry-lib.mjs): Paths for registry/sources and data/raw/registry; the Canary Wharf and Docklands boxes; cwPostcodes() (reads postcodes/postcodes.json; tiers cw-core, cw-ward, cw-box-other-ward, and terminated-cw unless told not to); postcode normaliser; CSV line splitter; sleep; inBox.
- [tools/osm-values.mjs](tools/osm-values.mjs): Parsers for OSM tag values: osmList (';' lists), unitLabel (adds 'Unit' to a bare designator), osmLevels (lists, comma lists, ranges, fractions) and osmLevelsUnread (tokens it cannot read).
- [registry/sources/brands/tools/lib.mjs](registry/sources/brands/tools/lib.mjs): Paths for the brands source (OUT, CW, RAW, NSI_DIR), the Canary Wharf box, One Canada Square, User-Agent, a require for the NSI packages installed in data/raw/registry/nsi, readJSON, sleep, inBox.
- [registry/sources/brands/tools/nsi-matcher.mjs](registry/sources/brands/tools/nsi-matcher.mjs): At import, builds the NSI Matcher over the brands/* tree with its location index (reads data/raw/registry/nsi/.../dist/json/nsi.json and featureCollection.json) and loads registry/sources/brands/brands-uk.json; exports matcher, brands, byId, byWd, KVS, hits(), nameVariants(), matchAny().
- [registry/sources/brands/tools/cwg-fields.mjs](registry/sources/brands/tools/cwg-fields.mjs): cwgFields (mall, level, postcode, district, street line and flags from CWG address lines), cwgLevelNum, nameKeys (name keys for joins: no accents or punctuation, "&" as "and", branch suffixes that name a place removed), CWG_PLACES, CWG_MALLS.
- [tools/structured-dom.mjs](tools/structured-dom.mjs): extractStructuredData(): self-contained in-page extractor for Playwright page.evaluate: JSON-LD script texts as found, HTML microdata as JSON (WHATWG algorithm, itemref followed), RDFa 1.1 (Core + HTML+RDFa rules) as N-Triples with the document base.
- [tools/jsonld-clean.mjs](tools/jsonld-clean.mjs): cleanJsonLd(raw): repairs one real-world JSON-LD block and counts each repair by class (HTML comment and CDATA wrappers, JS comments, trailing and missing commas, raw control characters, invalid escapes, concatenated values, HTML entities, remote schema.org contexts inlined, other remote contexts dropped, @graph unwrapped, http://schema.org/ normalised).
- [tools/native/ecw2ppm.c](tools/native/ecw2ppm.c): ECW decoder for build-aerial.py: decodes an ECW file to a PPM at every Nth pixel and prints its georeferencing as JSON. Built by hand against the ECW 3.3 SDK (curation skill, Imagery); the binary tools/native/ecw2ppm is gitignored.
- [tools/test/osm-values.test.mjs](tools/test/osm-values.test.mjs) (test): Fixture tests (node --test) for osmList, unitLabel, osmLevels and osmLevelsUnread in tools/osm-values.mjs.
- [tools/splat-pose-test.py](tools/splat-pose-test.py) (test): Pose-convention test for the 3DGS trainer: 'gen OUT' writes a synthetic nerfstudio dataset of coloured boxes on a chequered ground with known OpenGL camera-to-world matrices (images, transforms.json, sparse_pc.ply; --init grey, --flip opencv as a negative control); 'points OUT N' rewrites sparse_pc.ply with about N points; 'check PLY' reports per box how many opaque gaussians of a trained PLY lie inside it and their mean colour. Needs numpy and Pillow.
- [tools/test/cwg-fields.test.mjs](tools/test/cwg-fields.test.mjs) (test): Fixture tests (node --test) for cwgFields, cwgLevelNum and nameKeys in registry/sources/brands/tools/cwg-fields.mjs, including the parse faults of the first fetch-cwg ("United Kingdom" and "55 Upper Bank Street" as levels).
- [tools/test/jsonld-clean.test.mjs](tools/test/jsonld-clean.test.mjs) (test): Fixture tests (node --test) for cleanJsonLd in tools/jsonld-clean.mjs, including the bigeasy.co.uk missing-comma block of 2026-10-03.
