## TM-4: Wikidata occupants and headquarters without dates

Class: currency (timeliness). Method: Occupant (P466) and headquarters (P159) links used in the registry. The query fetched no start or end time qualifiers, so a former tenant cannot be told from a current one (for example the Financial Services Authority, abolished in 2013, is listed at One Canada Square).

Compositing rule: Fetch P580/P582 qualifiers and the organisation's dissolution date; treat links without dates as "at some time".

## SE-1: Registered offices concentrated at one address

Class: meaning (relevance). Method: Postcodes with 100 or more registered companies, and residential buildings with 20 or more. A registered office is a legal address: formation agents, accountants and serviced offices hold thousands.

Compositing rule: Never read a registered office as an occupant. Composite "who works here" from occupancy sources (OSM, FSA, CWG, business rates when licensed); join Companies House only for identity and status.
