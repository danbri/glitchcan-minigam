SPLATPACK · STAMP CATALOG

HOW TO PLAY

Left half of the screen: touch and drag — a floating stick walks you down the lanes. In the maze you KEEP moving, Pac-Man style: flick a direction at a junction to turn; a wall is the only brake.
Right half: drag to spin the camera. Pinch to zoom.
JUMP hops — you cannot be caught mid-air. Walk over 💎 to bank gems; ⭐ at the corners are worth a fanfare; 🔮 turns you into your chosen beast for two minutes and the citizens lose your scent.
The ☰ menu holds your beast, reset, this screen, and the workbench. ✕ SURFACE returns to the Skydock.
