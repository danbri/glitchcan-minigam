About

Elliott 4130 emulator based on E6X2/E6X3 specifications from
Computer Conservation Society (ourcomputerheritage.org).

24-bit word, two's complement. Registers: M (acc), R (reserve),
S (PC, half-word), K (count), C (conditions).

Test suite validates against E6X3 documented instruction behavior.
