foafos apps demo

Three spreadsheets and two stories open at the same time, and one Playlist that both stories use.

Open the demo

It opens five windows. On a phone, each window fills the screen: use the window list to move between them.

What to try

  1. Three spreadsheets. The Data app opens three times, on the documents budget, trips and reading. Each window has its own store, so a change in one does not appear in the others.
  2. One Playlist for two stories. In the first story, choose "Add 'The Quiet Engines' to the playlist". The Playlist opens and plays. The story does not wait for it. (If the browser blocks the sound, press Play in the Playlist.)
  3. In the second story, add a track. It goes to the same Playlist, after the first track, with the name of the story that added it. There is never a second player.
  4. A story inside a story. In the first story, choose "Go into a story inside this one". A ruled frame round the text and a line at the top show how deep you are. The outer story is suspended until the inner one ends.

What this demo replaces

This page was the runner for W3C MiniApp packages. The W3C stopped the MiniApp drafts in August 2026, and they do not meet what foafos needs: full apps with delegated capabilities, a message bus, RDF data and single-instance apps. foafos keeps the parts of MiniApps that fit and changes the rest.
