read from 17 - multiply - store in 47. The commands are then
perforated automatically by the machine. There is also a mode of
operation which allows direct execution of commands by triggering
the code keys. The list of codes will be presented.

The memory unit will be described in greater detail, as its
principle is very well adapted to relay calculators of medium
size.

The Z4 calculator has been running 10 hours per day since
August 1950 at our Institute. In addition to many secondary problems,
the following calculations of greater importance have been successfully
completed: Matrix inversion up to the 16th order; integration
of a system of eight simultaneous ordinary linear differential equations;
calculation of maxima of a family of functions with five parameters.
The calculation of function tables by means of continued fraction
developments is receiving special attention. The most important work
accomplished so far has been the integration of the partial differential
equation Delta-Delta u = 0 for the Airy stress function
in the profile of a dam, taking into account the elastic foundation
in a half-plane. The mathematical formulation will be briefly
outlined and it will be shown how one is reduced to 139 linear equations.
The relaxation technique is then applied; the Z4 calculator
automatically solves successive blocks of 16 linear equations.
Convergence can be accelerated by means of eigenfunction relaxation:
after an approximate calculation of the first function,
the contribution of this function to the residual error is made
to disappear.

## Notes
- The Z4 was operational at ETH Zurich from 1950-1955
- Matrix inversion and differential equations were key applications
- The Airy stress function is used in structural mechanics analysis
- "Relaxation technique" refers to iterative numerical methods developed by Southwell
- The 139-equation system for dam analysis shows the scale of problems being tackled
