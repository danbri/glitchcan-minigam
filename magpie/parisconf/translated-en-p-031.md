# Page 31

## Type
Abstract

## Language
French

## Translation

A. GONZALEZ del VALLE

THE TRIPOLE:

its use for the instantaneous resolution
of certain trigonometric problems

By considering the tripole as an assembly of three points
electrically connected to each other, the circuits being able to include
generators and receivers between each pair of points, relations have
been found, between the quantities attached to the tripole, identical
to those which link the angles and sides of a triangle.

This property is used to solve triangles electrically; and the
use of this procedure is obviously very interesting for problems
which require the successive resolution of a very large number of
these triangles, for example to record the trajectory of a projectile,
a sounding balloon, or some other moving object.

AUTOMATIC DETERMINATION OF THE POSITION AND TRAJECTORY

OF A TRANSMITTER IN SPACE

Starting from the homeomorphism between the plane triangle and
the electrical tripole, an electrical network has been designed which
performs by itself the triangulations necessary to determine the
projection of a mobile transmitter in space onto the terrestrial
surface.

The trajectory of this projection can remain recorded on the
screen of an oscillograph.

## Notes
- A. Gonzalez del Valle was from Madrid, presenting an analog computing technique
- The "tripole" is an electrical analog computer for solving triangulation problems
- This is a specialized analog device for tracking applications (missiles, weather balloons)
- Triangulation was essential for artillery, navigation, and early missile guidance
- The approach represents analog computing's strength: continuous, real-time computation of specific problems
- [Translation note: "homeomorphism" is a mathematical term for structural equivalence - here the electrical network topology mirrors triangle geometry, enabling analog solution of trigonometric problems]
