# Page 9

## Type
Administrative information

## Language
French

## Translation

- 2 -

SUBSCRIPTION TO THE PROCEEDINGS. In order to ensure a sufficient print run
of the Colloquium Proceedings, it is particularly important that Colloquium
Members indicate the number of copies they wish to receive before the end of
the Colloquium, by submitting the attached subscription form to the Secretariat
(Miss Franchino).

MISCELLANEOUS ARRANGEMENTS

_______________

BOOK EXHIBITION AND SALE. An exhibition of works relating to the
Colloquium proceedings will be held in the Secretariat room.
      The exhibited works may be purchased on site.

COMMUNAL MEALS. With the intention of bringing Colloquium members
closer together and allowing them to become better acquainted, a communal
lunch is organized from Monday 8th to Friday 12th.
      Organizational details will be posted at the Colloquium
Secretariat.
      Ladies accompanying Colloquium members are admitted to the
communal lunch.

BANQUET. The closing banquet will take place on Saturday 13 January,
at the Hotel School, 20 rue Mederic, Paris 17th.
      Colloquium members who have not yet registered for this
banquet may register at the Colloquium Secretariat (Miss Franchino).
      Ladies accompanying Colloquium members are admitted to the
banquet.
      Business attire.

EXCURSIONS. The Committee regrets being unable to organize all the
planned excursions, due to an insufficient number of requests.
      The outings that will take place are posted at the Colloquium
Secretariat, with the necessary information.
      Registrations may still be made for these outings.
Apply to the Colloquium Secretariat (Miss Franchino).

CORRESPONDENCE. Correspondence for Colloquium members may be
deposited at the Colloquium Secretariat, which will deliver it to the
recipients.
      For external mail: two post offices are located near
the National Centre for Pedagogical Documentation: one at the end of
rue d'Ulm, the other on rue Cujas.
      Refer to the map posted at the Colloquium Secretariat.

## Notes
- "Tenue de ville" (business attire) indicates formal but not black-tie dress code
- The communal lunches were a deliberate strategy to foster international scientific collaboration
- [Translation note: "Compte-rendus" is translated as "Proceedings" - the standard English term for published conference records]
