## Content

A. D. BOOTH

A MAGNETIC AUTOMATIC CALCULATING MACHINE

The two machines which have been constructed under the auspices of Birkbeck College, University of London will be described

The first one, a prototype called S.E.C., employs about 250 valves as storage for 256 6-decimal words but is without a multiplier.

The final model, A.P.E.X.C. has approximately 275 valves and has fully automatic multiplication and other facilities together with a storage capacity of 512 9-decimal words. Both machines employ a magnetic drum storage as a high speed memory.
