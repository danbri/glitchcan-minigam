The corresponding solutions in the case of an n-dimensional space are easily written.

The preceding method has the disadvantage of allowing only a single verification: it is necessary that, in integrating along a parallel to the t-axis, one obtains

u_t2 - u_t1 = integral from t1 to t2 of (du/dt) dt

A variant of the method permits more complete verifications; it consists in calculating first, in each plane parallel to the initial plane, the three derivatives

du/dx,     du/dy     and     du/dt,

and then forming the total differential

du = du/dx dx + du/dy dy + du/dt dt

One can then verify the values of u by comparing the values obtained by following different integration paths.
