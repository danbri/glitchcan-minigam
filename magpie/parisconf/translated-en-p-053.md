In his works on analytical machines my father always had in view the works of the great English scientist BABBAGE, but the solutions are different.

Several of these devices have been exhibited many times in Spain, France and Portugal. My father, in addition to these scientific works, developed other inventions touching on the art of the Engineer, notably dirigible balloons of which several, of the type invented by him, were in the service of the French and English armies during the first great war, and aerial cable-cars.

There are in Spain other inventors of calculating machines. At the moment of writing this summary I am not sufficiently informed. I hope to be able to give a slight overview of them in the presentation for the colloquium.

At least three devices will be presented at the colloquium: the endless screw (which is the most original part of the algebraic machines), the telekine tested at Bilbao and the second chess player. Perhaps, if there is possibility, I could present other devices belonging to my father or to other Spanish inventors.


PRESENTATION OF THE DEVICES of Leonardo TORRES-QUEVEDO

1. The automatic chess player
2. The telekine, first device constructed in
   the world for the radio-guidance of boats
3. The endless screw of the machine for solving algebraic equations


The automatic chess player.- This is the second of the 2 machines that my father had constructed as an amusing demonstration which catches attention. It proves that one can construct automata acting in appearance with discernment. It suffices at the moment of construction to specify the manner in which they must act in all cases, based on certain rules imposed in advance.

It would be practically impossible to construct a machine capable of playing a complete game of chess in a reasonable manner, that is to say like a player of average strength.
