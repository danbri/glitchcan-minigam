## Content

Jean PELTIER


CALCULATION OF CERTAIN COMMON FUNCTIONS IN THE BINARY SYSTEM


The use of electronic calculating machines operating in the binary system, which has already allowed M. Louis COUFFIGNAL to have these machines perform the operations of division and extraction of square roots in times sensibly equal to the duration of a multiplication, has led us to seek, for the calculation of logarithmic, exponential and power functions, methods using certain simple arithmetic properties of binary notation.

These methods consist of deducing from the given value x of the variable a neighbouring value x' for which the desired function is simply calculated, and of performing a limited development as a function of (x - x'), x' being chosen, moreover, in such a way that this development contains only a small number of terms. They lead to calculations which, in comparison with usual methods, seem at once to diminish the duration of calculations and the number of constant registers and memory that they require.

Numerical examples are cited.
