A. D. BOOTH

A MAGNETIC AUTOMATIC CALCULATING MACHINE

The two machines which have been constructed under the
auspices of Birkbeck College, University of London will be des-
cribed

The first one, a prototype called S.E.C., employs about
250 valves as storage for 256 6-decimal words but is without a
multiplier.

The final model, A.P.E.X.C. has approximately 275 val-
ves and has fully automatic multiplication and other facilities
together with a storage capacity of 512 9-decimal words. Both
machines employ a magnetic drum storage as a high speed memory.
