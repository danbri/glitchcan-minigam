## Notes
[Original in English]

This is page 2 of Dr. A. van Wijngaarden's paper on "Rounding-off Errors in Systematic Calculations."

The paper demonstrates counterintuitive results about numerical precision - cases where "non-significant figures" actually contain useful information, and where increasing precision in only part of a calculation can paradoxically destroy accuracy.
