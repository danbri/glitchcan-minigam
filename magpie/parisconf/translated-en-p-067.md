- "rythme alpha" - alpha rhythm, the 8-13 Hz brain waves discovered by Hans Berger in 1924
- [Translation note: Couffignal is proposing that the alpha rhythm serves as a "clock signal" for the brain, analogous to the clock in a calculating machine - an idea that would be debated for decades in neuroscience]
- "la pensée" translated as "thoughts" - period terminology; "cognition" as a term did not yet exist
- [Translation note: The proposal to compare "ideal logic" (what machines can achieve) with "real logic" (how humans actually reason) anticipates later work in cognitive science and behavioral economics on human reasoning biases]
