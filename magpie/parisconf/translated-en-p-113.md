## Notes
[Original in English]

This is page 3 of Gonzalo Torres-Quevedo's paper on "The Automatism Works of Spanish School."

[Translation note: "the great English scientist Babbage" - Charles Babbage (1791-1871), designer of the Difference Engine and Analytical Engine. Torres-Quevedo knew Babbage's work well but developed independent solutions.]

[Translation note: The "endless fuze" (also written "endless fuse") was an ingenious mechanical component for analog computation of algebraic equations.]

[Translation note: Torres-Quevedo's dirigible airships were indeed used in WWI - the "Astra-Torres" design was licensed to France and Britain.]
