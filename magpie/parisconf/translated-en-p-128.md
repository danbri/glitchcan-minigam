## End of Conference Proceedings

This concludes the 128-page proceedings of the International Colloquium on Computing Machines and Human Thought, held at the Institut Blaise Pascal (CNRS), Paris, 8-13 January 1951.

The conference brought together leading figures in computing, mathematics, neurophysiology, and psychology to explore the relationships between mechanical calculation and human cognition - five years before the field of "Artificial Intelligence" would be named at the Dartmouth Conference, and decades before "cognitive science" emerged as a unified discipline.
