COLLOQUIUM ORGANIZING COMMITTEE

Chairman

Mr. Joseph PERES

      Misses M.A. LENOUVEL

            D. ROLLAND
            J. GRUPINSKI
      Mrs.  D. RAKINSKI
      Mr.   E. BALKIN


The Organizing Committee wishes to associate with its members the external
collaborators outside the C.N.R.S. whose obliging and disinterested assistance
has been particularly valuable, notably:

      The National Centre for Pedagogical Documentation, which provides the
session and exhibition rooms,

      The Estienne School (School of Book Arts), which produced the
printed pieces of documentation distributed to members of the Colloquium,

      The Colombes Printing Apprenticeship Centre, responsible for
the folder covers,

      The Gauging and Scaling Society, creator of the individual
badges,

      The Logabax Company, which ensures the recording of discussions
on the Audibel magnetic recorder,

      The Paris Hotel School, where the Colloquium concludes with its
Closing Banquet.

## Notes
- Joseph Peres was a French mathematician and member of the Academy of Sciences
- Louis Couffignal was the director of the Institut Blaise Pascal and a pioneer of French computing
- The Ecole Estienne, founded 1889, is a prestigious French school for printing and graphic arts
- Logabax was a French business machines company; the Audibel was an early magnetic tape recorder
- [Translation note: "Mlles" = Mesdemoiselles (unmarried women); "Mme" = Madame (married woman) - these formal distinctions were standard in 1951 French]
