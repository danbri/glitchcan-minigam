## Type

## Language

## Content

M. V. WILKES


EXPERIENCE OF OPERATIONS PERFORMED WITH THE E.D.S.A.C.


The article will contain an account of the experience that has been obtained in using the E.D.S.A.C. for the solution of mathematical problems, since it began to function in summer 1949. We shall examine in particular the manner in which orders are inscribed on perforated tape and the filing of these documents in a library. Some remarks will be made on the subject of the organization necessary for the machine to satisfy at once the needs of a certain number of different users.

---

## Notes
- Author shown as "M. V. WILKES" (Maurice Vincent Wilkes)
- E.D.S.A.C. refers to the Electronic Delay Storage Automatic Calculator at Cambridge
- [Translation note: EDSAC, which began operating in May 1949, was one of the first stored-program calculating machines. Wilkes's discussion of "library" documents for orders foreshadows the development of software libraries and programming practices. The concern about multiple users sharing a machine anticipates time-sharing systems.]
