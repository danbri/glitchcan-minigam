In Gothenburg we have constructed a mechanical differential analyser of the Bush type with five integrators, which presents some novel aspects. In particular, the integrators are of a construction which has allowed us to completely avoid torque amplifiers. These rather delicate devices had previously been necessary in this type of machine.

At Chalmers Technical University, an electrical differential analyser of the Wallman-Macnee type has also been completed.

Among other projects presenting interesting aspects, the following may be mentioned: an electrolytic tank (L. Stenstrom) for aerodynamics problems; another (G. Svala) for the automatic plotting of electron trajectories in electric and magnetic fields; electrical machines for solving algebraic equations (L. Lofgren), for the analysis and synthesis of Fourier series (G. Hagg and T. Laurent), and for the calculation of Fourier transforms (E. Persson).
