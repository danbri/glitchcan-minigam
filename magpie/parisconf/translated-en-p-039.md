## Content

F. PUIG ADAM


LAPLACE TRANSFORMS OF EMPIRICAL FUNCTIONS


Theoretical and practical comparative exposition of various means for obtaining the Laplace transform of a function f(x) given by experimental measurements. The transformation parameter being supposed at first to be real, various analytical expressions of these transforms are formulated with a view to extrapolating the results in the complex field. We consider the errors resulting from the approximations made under the integral sign as well as those which arise from the substitution of a sufficiently large finite interval for the infinite interval, within which one operates. In the case where f(x) is given by isolated measurements, the approximations used under the integral sign are: a) Approximations by entire polynomials, in particular those of minimum approximation of Chebyshev; b) Approximations by exponential polynomials; c) Mean approximations, especially with Fourier polynomials. Two simple graphical and graphomechanical procedures are indicated, applicable to the case where the function is obtained by an experimental graph, making use of an ordinary planimeter or an integraph. The results of the methods are compared on a numerical example.
