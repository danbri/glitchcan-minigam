## Translation Notes

- C.N.R.S. - Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique (National Centre for Scientific Research), France's principal governmental research organization
- "Grosses Machines à Calculer" - literally "Large Calculating Machines," the period term for what would later be called mainframe computers or simply computers
- [Translation note: The term "ordinateur" (the modern French word for computer) was not coined until 1955 by Jacques Perret at IBM France's request]
- Handwritten annotation "engl." appears below "1ère Section," indicating that the abstracts in this section were presented in English
- This page serves as a section divider for the conference proceedings
