## Notes
- C.N.R.S. = Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique (National Centre for Scientific Research)
- Louis de Broglie was a Nobel Prize-winning physicist (1929, wave-particle duality)
- Several presentation titles were originally in English, reflecting the international character of the conference
- [Translation note: "machines à calculer" is rendered as "calculating machines" - the French word "ordinateur" for computer would not be coined until 1955]
