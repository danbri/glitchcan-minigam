The homeostat demonstrates the properties of such a second-order feedback. Four elements representing the brain and its surroundings in connection with each other are constrained to act in such a way that these elements maintain four needles in central position, corresponding to the maintenance of four physiological variables at their optimal values. The operator can modify various parameters and operating conditions in the part representing the surroundings and thus demonstrate that, independently of the modification of the "surroundings", the "brain" will find a series of parameter values which will re-establish homeostasis.

Thus, the machine shows some of the fundamental biological properties of the living brain and suggests that, perhaps, the living brain can use second-order feedbacks in its self-regulation activities.

The device influenced both cybernetics and early artificial intelligence thinking.
