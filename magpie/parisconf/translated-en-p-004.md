## Notes
- E.D.S.A.C. = Electronic Delay Storage Automatic Calculator (Cambridge, UK)
- B.A.R.K. = Binar Automatisk RelaeKalkylator (Swedish relay computer)
- D. R. Hartree was a pioneering British mathematician and physicist
- M. V. Wilkes led the EDSAC project and would later receive the Turing Award (1967)
- Louis Lapicque was a renowned French neurophysiologist
- W. Grey Walter was a British neurophysiologist known for his robotic "tortoises"
- [Translation note: "erreurs de chute" literally "fall errors" refers to rounding/truncation errors in computation]
