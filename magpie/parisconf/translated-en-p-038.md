Mauro PICONE


EXPOSITION OF A METHOD OF NUMERICAL INTEGRATION

OF SYSTEMS OF LINEAR PARTIAL DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS

implemented at the National Institute for the Applications of Calculus

Results obtained and results that could be achieved.


In the nearly quarter century of its activity, the Italian Institute for the Applications of Calculus has always worked on problems concerning the integration of systems of linear partial differential equations, posed by Physics or by Technology, with the double aim of providing existence theorems and methods for the numerical calculation of solutions.

In the necessity of responding with a certain precision to questions posed by the civil construction industry, bridges, large dams, ..., it has had to consider, thoroughly, the problem of calculating the stresses to which the said structures are subjected in the vicinity of their embeddings in supports of great rigidity, of which an imperfect knowledge is often the cause - as is well known - of dangerous cracks.

This problem presents serious difficulties, for it is a matter of calculating the values of certain expressions composed of the partial derivatives of the solution of a boundary problem, at the frontier of the domain of existence of the solution itself. Now, generally, one has been able to ensure the convergence of ordinary methods of calculating the solution and some of its derivatives, only in the interior of the said domain, and such convergence is often far - as all analysts well know - from lending itself to numerical calculation in the proximity of the frontier!

We expose a direct method of numerical calculation of stresses at the embeddings of elastic solids, followed at the Italian Institute for the Applications of Calculus, the results obtained in some remarkable particular cases and the results that could be achieved, in general, if one succeeded in constructing calculating machines capable of solving a very large number of linear algebraic equations.
