SECRETARIAT

_______________

COLLOQUIUM SECRETARIAT. During the Colloquium, the Secretariat of the
Colloquium Organizing Committee will be located at the National Centre
for Pedagogical Documentation, 29 rue d'Ulm, Paris 5th, in the Ferdinand
BUISSON room, to the right of the main entrance, and not at the Institut
Blaise Pascal.

      Administrative Secretariat: Miss FRANCHINO
      Scientific Organization: Miss SOULE-NAN
      Material Organization: Miss LENOUVEL

COLLOQUIUM PROCEEDINGS

_______________

WORKING SESSIONS. The Colloquium sessions will take place at the National
Centre for Pedagogical Documentation, in the Jules FERRY room, according to the
attached programme.

      All modifications to the programme as well as all information
of interest to Colloquium members will be posted at the Secretariat.

STUDY MEETINGS. Study meetings among Colloquium members may be organized
during the Colloquium. Requests should be made to the Colloquium Secretariat
(Miss Lenouvel).

PERSONAL INVITATIONS. Colloquium members may have invitations sent to
persons of their choice for a particular Colloquium session.
Apply to the Colloquium Secretariat (Miss Franchino).

PUBLICATION OF COLLOQUIUM PROCEEDINGS. Authors of communications are
urgently requested to submit the text of their communication (in French
or English) to the Section Secretary at the end of their communication.

      It is only under this condition that the publication of the Colloquium
proceedings can be completed within normal timeframes.

      In the event that they cannot submit at the same time and in final
form a copy of each of the figures, they are requested to make arrangements
with the Colloquium Secretariat (Miss Soule-Nan), so that a photographic
reproduction may be made before the end of the Colloquium.

## Notes
- 29 rue d'Ulm is in the Latin Quarter, near the Ecole Normale Superieure
- Ferdinand Buisson (1841-1932) was a French educator and Nobel Peace Prize laureate
- Jules Ferry (1832-1893) was a French statesman who established free, secular public education in France
