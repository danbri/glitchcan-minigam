## Translation from French

- 12 -

I have just presented. But there exist many other elements that have an important role in the spinal cord. For example, many interneurons that, by relaying impulses coming through descending systems, will prolong the action of impulses coming through the spinal roots. Our experiments were carried out on the spinal or decerebrate cat, but even under these conditions, which avoid using a general anesthetic, very weak stimulations are necessary to obtain images uncomplicated by interneuron activity, for the purpose of demonstrating these two types of inhibition, one depending on changes induced at the level of the motoneuron cell, the other depending on an interaction of presynaptic elements. I would not want you to think that the physiology of the nervous system has just been born. Let me express it thus. We already have enough results to be able to calculate what happens to impulses traveling in axons, but the solution of the equations is so laborious that we need a calculating machine to obtain information even on just a few parameters.

We must also calculate the probability for a wave of impulses to excite a cell after it has traveled through a series of dense arborizations bifurcating at random and strewn with possible inhibitions created by its intermingling with a sparse arborization terminating in that place from another system.

The problem is the same as that of evaluating the chances that a volley of guided missiles has of sinking a ship despite the counter-fire trying to intercept them.

Last figure:

It often happens that one believes one has a theory until the moment when one has made such calculations and one realizes it does not withstand more precise evaluations.

Likewise, I do not doubt that at this moment you are seeing spirals where there are circles. This is what you would discover by forcing yourself to trace them with your fingers.

## Translation Notes

- "chat spinal ou décérébré" - spinal or decerebrate cat, experimental preparations where the spinal cord is severed or the brain is removed to study spinal reflexes in isolation
- [Translation note: McCulloch's military analogy (guided missiles vs. counter-fire) reflects the wartime origins of cybernetics and its connections to operations research]
- [Translation note: The "last figure" with illusory spirals demonstrates that perception involves computation that can be fooled - what we see is not raw sensory data but processed interpretation]
- [Translation note: McCulloch's call for calculating machines to solve neurophysiological equations presages the later use of computer simulations in neuroscience]
