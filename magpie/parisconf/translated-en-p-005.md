## Notes
- Leonardo Torres Quevedo (1852-1936) was a Spanish engineer and mathematician; his son Gonzalo presented his father's work
- The "automatic chess player" was an electromechanical endgame player (King and Rook vs King) from 1912
- W. R. Ashby's homeostat was an early cybernetic device demonstrating self-organizing behavior
- W. Grey Walter's "artificial animals" were his famous robotic tortoises (Elmer and Elsie)
- N. Wiener was the founder of cybernetics; his book "Cybernetics" had just been published in 1948
- W. S. McCulloch co-authored the foundational 1943 paper on neural networks with Walter Pitts
- [Translation note: "la machine nerveuse" (the nervous machine) reflects the cybernetic view of the nervous system as a type of machine - a conceptual framework central to this conference]
