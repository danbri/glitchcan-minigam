The here proposed method consists in the determination of the u and du/dt values in a plane parallel to the initial plane. If this plane is taken as a new initial plane, the Cauchy's problem can be resolved again in order to go in a third plane, and so on.

The proceed is extended to the case of the equation including a second member and terms such as

d^2 u / (d x_i d x_j),   d^2 u / (d x_1 d t),   du/dx_i,   du/dt   and   u.

The case when the coefficients are variable, can be brought back to the precedent one, by dividing the integration domain into rather small regions.
