Demo 3 — the stage: pose → packet → lossy net → splat viewer

hub · packet · room

The whole thesis in one page. The sender's pose is encoded into 32-byte packets and pushed through a simulated lossy channel; the viewer decodes, jitter-buffers, interpolates and (briefly) extrapolates, and renders the splat room + splat avatar. Turn the loss and jitter up and watch the avatar stay coherent. Network and capture are simulated in-page (DESIGN.md §6). The second figure in the room is a 100Avatars model by Polygonal Mind (CC BY 4.0), splat-ified from its VRM at load time.
