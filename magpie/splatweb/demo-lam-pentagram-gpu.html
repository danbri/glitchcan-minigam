Pentulpa Capulet — GPU

same five-persona creature, WGSL compute pipeline
A "safe copy" — this is a NEW file, demo-lam-pentagram.html (the CPU version) is untouched. Persona switching, per-persona tint/ stylize, voices, subtitles, turn SFX, and real blink + speech lipsync (17 ARKit morph targets blended on GPU, active-face only) are all real. Arrow keys move between faces. Switching spins the whole pentagon to face wherever the camera currently is, with a spring overshoot-and- judder settle; two hops at once (or firing another switch within 0.6s) makes the spin quicker and more violent, with a temporary twinkle/ orbit-particle boost on every facet while it's in flight. The orbit- particle effect: every splat, at its own phase, spirals out around the shared pole on a slow, noise-wobbled path, grows by a perlin-per-splat amount (occasionally much more), sparkles, and while the creature is mid-spin the currently-launched ("free") splats lag the rigid rotation by a perlin-varied ~200ms before catching up — a cheap hair-like secondary-motion trail — then swirls back home. All of this is computed in the same compute shader (lib/gpu-skinned-avatar.js). NOT ported: the CPU version's medusa-fling secondary motion and the mist shroud — no cloud/core base under the heads yet.
