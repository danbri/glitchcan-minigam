Demo 2 — the room avatar (WebGL2 splats)

hub · packet · stage

A procedural stand-in for a scanned + stylised room bundle: ~3,000 Gaussian splats, rendered by a dependency-free WebGL2 rasterizer (covariance projection, eigen ellipses, CPU depth sort). Drag to orbit, wheel/pinch to zoom. The stylise slider quantizes the palette in-shader — a taste of the runtime end of the clean/simplify/stylise pipeline.
