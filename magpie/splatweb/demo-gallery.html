Demo 4 — borrowed models, splat-ified live

hub · catalogue · stage

Openly licensed models vendored in third_party/, converted to Gaussian splats in the browser at load time: the GLB/VRM triangles are area-sampled, the base-color texture is read at each point, and each sample becomes a surface-tangent splat with baked lighting — rendered by the same renderer as everything else. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.
