Integration sketch (DESIGN.md phase 3): FaceLandmarker runs in a worker → its transformation matrix becomes head quat+position, 10 of its 52 blendshape scores fill the packet → the existing encoder, network, buffer and renderers are untouched. A VRM avatar (Tier 2) consumes the same packet through its humanoid bone + morph-target names. The wire format is the contract; everything else is swappable.
