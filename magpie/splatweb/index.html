splatweb — presence, not pixels

Stream a 32-byte pose packet at 30 Hz plus voice, instead of video, and let viewers reconstruct you inside a cached Gaussian-splat room. ~40 kbit/s instead of ~1,500 — and fan-out to thousands with no transcode. DESIGN.md has the full design (original draft); the catalogue lists every borrowed model and capture stack with its licence checked.

DEMO 1

The 32-byte packet

Live hex view of the wire format: drive a face, watch it encode and decode, read the quantization error and the bandwidth-vs-video meter.
DEMO 2

The room avatar

A procedural room rendered by the dependency-free WebGL2 Gaussian-splat rasterizer. Orbit, zoom, try the stylise slider.
DEMO 3

The stage — full pipeline

Pose → packet → simulated lossy network → jitter buffer → splat room + rigged splat avatar. Thirteen rooms. Real capture via 🎤 MIC and 📷 CAM. Crank the loss and watch presence survive.
DEMO 4

Borrowed models, splat-ified

Open models (a CC BY head scan, the CC BY 100Avatars roster) converted to Gaussian splats in-browser at load time.
DEMO 5

Critter room

Jelly critters with squash-and-stretch physics and per-critter voices, hopping around a room built from licence-proven splat stamps.
DEMO 6

Rig lab — skinned splat avatars

The VRM roster as live splat rigs: CPU skinning + expression morphs, stored clips, two-bone arm IK, skeleton control points. Where the avatar look gets decided.
DEMO 7 experimental

LAM Gaussian head avatar

A real trained head — genuine per-splat rotation, scale and colour instead of area-sampled fake splats — skinned and driven by 51 ARKit expression morphs. Local test page: console-only controls, no UI yet, front-facing only (no back-of-head data).
DEMO 8 experimental

54 synthetic faces, mix & match

Two independent carousels — 43 curated public-domain StyleGAN2 faces × 21 dbdb foliage treatments — plus a tunable ghost/particle stylize pass (round-particle shape, secondary-action lag, noise dissolve, twinkle, jitter) built to close the look gap between the photoreal faces and the lower-fidelity foliage "hair". Mobile-first collapsible UI.
DEMO 9 experimental

Head in a jar — art direction

Same LAM heads, two competing containment concepts compared side by side: a shimmering cold-flame orb, and a Victorian glass bell jar (real fresnel/screen-space refraction) on a rough oak slab. Both hide LAM's hollow back-of-head by design, not by luck.
DEMO 10 experimental

Layer compositor — splats + SDF + three.js

A small node-edge dataflow graph (lib/layer-compositor.js) for combining heterogeneous layers into one frame: a raymarched SDF pass, a three.js mesh, and a splat avatar, blended together. Webcam and media-file layers are implemented too (lib/layers.js) — this demo just doesn't force a camera grant on load.
DEMO 11 experimental

GPU splat compute — avatar + critters, one pipeline

A skinned LAM head and 14 physics-driven jelly critters — including live ear-flop and mouth/jaw — animated entirely by WGSL compute shaders instead of the CPU per-splat loops the rest of this project uses. lib/gpu-splat- compute.js is the shared scaffolding both content types plug into; same asset loaders and same render shader as everywhere else, unmodified. See the file header for the honest scope of what's not ported yet.
DEMO 12 experimental

Pentulpa Capulet, rebuilt on GPU compute

A "safe copy" of demo-lam-pentagram.html's five-persona creature — the CPU original is untouched — running on the WGSL compute pipeline: real persona switching, per-persona tint/dissolve/ twinkle/roundness, voices, subtitles, and turn SFX all reused. 100,090 splats at 60fps here, versus the CPU version's ~20fps at 67,489 splats. Not ported: the spring-overshoot spin, the medusa-fling secondary motion, and the mist/core shroud.

Honesty box: capture and network are simulated in-page; most rooms are procedural, not scans; no XMPP yet. Real: the wire format, the receiver model, the splat renderer, and (new) the LAM avatar's splat data. See DESIGN.md §6.
