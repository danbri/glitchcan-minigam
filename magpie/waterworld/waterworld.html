🧭 HOW TO CAPTAIN
  • THE STORY: your first banking wakes the bergs. Build the coalition of three — whale ghosts (their bones → Steelyard → loudspeaker → sink the candle barge), the eel tribes (parley → find the torn charter), and the river (the East India rubies: spend or return). Then herd the risen bergs to Blight Corner and spark the pylon.
  • Drag the water — you are holding her nose: drag right to turn right, drag DOWN to dive, UP to climb. She holds the course, then resumes the hunt.
  • Tap the water — sonar ping: lights up treasure, stuns eels up close, pops mines at a safe distance. Pinged things stay on your 🗺 chart.
  • Press and HOLD still — hold station (the brake). Release to sail on.
  • Tap the goal banner — the Coalition board: every task, its progress, the seats filled.
  • Double-tap (or double-tap A) — DASH: a burst of speed for a breath of air.
  • 🔔 BANK — the haul multiplier grows with every item you carry (×3 tops!) — but a hit knocks half your cargo loose. Banking is YOUR call.
  • Grab fast for COMBOS — quick successive finds are worth more.
  • Watch the water — tides surge the current, and sometimes the silt exposes a cache for sixty seconds.
  • ➤ The banner arrow always points at your current goal.
  • ⚓AUTO — autopilot on/off. Off = you have the boat.
  • 🎮 — summon the d-pad if you want stick-and-button control (▲▼◀▶ steer · A thrust · B sonar/tools).
  • IR — thermal sight: fatbergs and mines glow warm through the dark. Ghosts run cold.
  • 📜 — the ship's log keeps every fact and event of the dive.
  • Swim into shiny things to collect them; the glowing bell banks cargo and refills air.
  • Pink glows are gear — two parts auto-craft a tool: grapple, arc lamp, fizz lance (hold B by a fatberg).
  • ⛶ — fullscreen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen runs it truly fullscreen.
  • Keyboard: WASD/arrows · Space thrust · Esc/X sonar · I thermal.
