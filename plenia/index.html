Particle Lenia is an artificial life simulation where simple rules create complex, life-like behavior.

1. Sensing: Each particle detects neighbors within a radius and measures local "density" (U).

2. Growth: Particles are attracted to their ideal density. Too sparse or too crowded = unhappy. Just right = growth (G).

3. Repulsion: Overlapping particles push apart (R).

4. Movement: Particles flow down the energy gradient: E = R − G. They move toward lower energy states.

The balance between growth and repulsion creates self-organizing structures that resemble living organisms.
