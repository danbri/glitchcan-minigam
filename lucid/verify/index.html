🔬 Lucid Visual Verification — what does it really look like on your device?

Headless test machines can't render WebGPU and barely render WebGL, so an automated agent never actually sees Lucid on real hardware. This page turns your real browser into its eyes: it shows each scene in both backends, asks you what you see, and packages your answers (plus screenshots) into a GitHub issue the agent can read back.

Look at as many or as few scenes as you like — there's nothing to complete. Open any scene to compare the two backends (onion overlay, deviation lab, rotate & animate). Filing a report is optional — only flag the ones where something looks off, then submit just those.

Your report is below. It includes a machine-readable block the agent parses automatically — your typed answers are the real signal, screenshots are a corroborating record.

How to finish:

  1. Click Open pre-filled GitHub issue — title, labels and your answers are filled in.

  3. Submit the issue. That's it — the agent reads it from there.
