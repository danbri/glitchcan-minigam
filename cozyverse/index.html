Steeple Wyke: The Marrow Show

A village cosy mystery over ten pages. The brief: every panel photorealistic, and every panel with its own sound over the page's sound.

  • Three layers of sound: a bed for each page, a sound for each panel, and voices that lower both.
  • Each loop shows one small event (a wasp, a rook landing).

Owner's verdict: "very linear"; "voices sometimes v flat"; "some scenes flicker visibly as it loops".

  • 10 pages
  • 40 panels
  • 10 looping panels
  • 130 voiced lines

Steeple Wyke: Plain Hunt

A bell-ringing mystery. One bell in the tower was silent; the ringers' rows tell you which.

  • A puzzle with two routes to the answer, and a checker that proves the answer is the only one.
  • The village remembers what you said to people, from one reading to the next.
  • Subtitles: the text in Italian over the English voices.
  • Longer loops: rooks round the tower, the pub fire, wind in the lane, steam from a kettle, a swing.
  • 10 pages
  • 40 panels
  • 7 looping panels
  • 229 voiced lines
  • English, Italian
