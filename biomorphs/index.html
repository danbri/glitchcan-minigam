Biomorphs

Faithful JavaScript port of Richard Dawkins' Monochrome WatchMaker biomorph drawing algorithm (The Blind Watchmaker, 1986; v1.1 Pascal source 1993). The original Pascal lives at third_party/monochrome_pascal/; the runtime is biomorph.js. Click any non-parent cell to make it the new parent and breed a fresh litter of mutants.
