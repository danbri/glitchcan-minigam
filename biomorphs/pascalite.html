Biomorphs (Pascal-lite — Dawkins' Pascal source executed live)

The runtime here is pascalite.js, a small Pascal interpreter written in JS. On load it fetch()es Globals and Biomorphs verbatim from third_party/monochrome_pascal/ and executes Develop() directly. Drawing routes through a host stub for PicLine straight onto a canvas. Click any non-parent cell to make it the new parent.
