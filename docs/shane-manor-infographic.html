THE MANOR HOUSE MYSTERY

Inspector Shane André-Louis Investigation - Interactive Fiction Summary

November, Victorian England. Lord Edgar Pemberton, 58, chess master and patriarch of Greystone Manor, is found stabbed in his locked study. A silver letter opener protrudes from between his shoulder blades. Inspector Shane André-Louis must navigate a web of family secrets, forbidden love, and a conspiracy spanning two decades to unmask the killer.

THE CAST

🔍 Inspector Shane André-Louis

The Detective (Player)

A methodical investigator who can employ psychological, observational, or procedural approaches. His reputation rises or falls based on player choices.

💀 Lord Edgar Pemberton

The Victim

Age 58. Chess master. Took in Victoria as ward 20 years ago. Recently discovered dangerous truths and changed his will one week before death.

He was hiding Victoria from her criminal father.

👩 Victoria Ashworth

Primary Suspect / Ward

Foundling raised at the manor. True identity: daughter of Viktor Markov, hidden since infancy. Disinherited days before the murder.

Lost inheritance, identity crisis, seen arguing with victim

🎰 Charles Pemberton

Suspect / Nephew

Lord Pemberton's nephew. Gambling debts to "nasty London types." Chain-smoker. Now primary beneficiary under new will.

Gambling debts, inheritance, humiliated at chess

👵 Mrs. Margaret Pemberton

Suspect / Widow

Watches Victoria "like a hawk." Takes sleeping draughts. Possibly embezzling from estate for years to pay Charles's debts.

Financial crimes exposed, protecting Charles

🎩 Ashford

Suspect / Butler

30 years of service. Secretly loved Elena (Victoria's mother). Arranged her escape from Markov. Knows all the manor's secret passages.

Protecting Victoria, honoring promise to Elena

🧹 Mary Collins

Witness / Maid

Age 25. Found the body at 8 AM. Heard the argument at midnight. Discovered Victoria's muddy boots and was sworn to secrecy.

🇷🇺 Viktor Markov

Hidden Threat / Victoria's Father

Russian criminal. "A monster." Has been searching for Victoria for 20 years. Recently discovered she survived the fire.

💔 Elena Markov (deceased)

Victoria's Mother

Died in Ashworth family fire. Loved by Ashford. Fled from Viktor. The "E.M." on the locked jewelry box.

🌙 James Blackwood

Mentioned Suspect

"Skulking around after dark" according to village gossip. Connection to the mystery remains unclear.

RELATIONSHIP WEB

  • Lord Pemberton → guardian of Victoria (20 years)
  • Lord Pemberton → owed debt to Ashford
  • Ashford → loved Elena, protects Victoria
  • Elena → fled Viktor, mother of Victoria
  • Viktor Markov → hunting for Victoria
  • Mrs. Pemberton → paid Charles's debts
  • Charles → gambling debts, resents Victoria

TIMELINE OF EVENTS

23 Years Ago: Victoria placed with Ashworth family. Elena dies in fire. Lord Pemberton takes Victoria as ward at Ashford's request.
3 Days Before Murder: Solicitor Hartwell warns Lord Pemberton that Markov's agents are asking questions.
1 Week Before Murder: Lord Pemberton changes will, disinherits Victoria entirely "to protect her."
Evening of Murder: Victoria finds old letters about her parentage. Confronts Lord Pemberton.
~11 PM: Victoria seen carrying letters, crying. Headed toward study. Mary hears argument: "I know who you really are."
Midnight-2 AM: Time of death. Chess game interrupted. Someone exits via hidden door.
8 AM: Mary Collins discovers body. Door apparently locked from inside.

THE CORE SECRET

  • Victoria is the daughter of Viktor Markov, a Russian criminal
  • Her mother Elena fled Viktor while pregnant
  • Ashford loved Elena and helped her escape
  • Lord Pemberton hid Victoria as a favor to Ashford
  • The will change was meant to make Victoria worthless as leverage against Markov
  • Someone in the house wanted both Lord Pemberton dead and Victoria blamed

POSSIBLE ENDINGS

Blood and Loyalty: Ashford confesses - killed to protect Victoria from being traded to Markov
The Prodigal Son: Charles confesses - killed in rage after chess humiliation
The Spider's Web: Mrs. Pemberton's embezzlement exposed, complicity suspected
The Broken Family: Victoria's heartbreak but not guilt - true killer unknown
Shadows of Doubt: Ambiguous - financial crimes cast doubt on all
The Long Shadow: External conspiracy - Markov's agents implicated
Family Sins: Multiple conspirators, no single killer
Unfinished Business: Case remains open
